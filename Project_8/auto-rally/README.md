# Auto Rally

A self-contained, offline-ready browser racing game against two AI rickshaw racers. No build step, libraries, or remote assets are needed.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Tap **Start the race**, then steer with the on-screen buttons or **← / →** (also **A / D**). Use **Turbo** or **Space** to spend a cup of chai. The pause button or **Esc** pauses the clock. Complete three arch crossings; steer near the middle when the arch arrives. A missed arch does not count, and the next lap gives you another chance.

Collect coins and chai, avoid potholes, cows, and rival autos. Three pixel hearts track your lives; every crash breaks a heart, and the race ends when all three are gone. Finish order shows a trophy for first place. **High Scores & Previous Times** on the home screen shows the five fastest and five most recent completed races. The pause menu and finish screen both have a **Back to home** option. Best time and up to 20 race results are saved in the browser on that device. Sound is synthesized locally; the music-note button mutes it.

The page, graphics, and sound work without a network connection. To play on another phone, copy this folder to the phone and open its `index.html`, or serve the folder as a static site and open it once before going offline. Browser settings that clear site data also clear the saved best time.
