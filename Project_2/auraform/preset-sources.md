# AuraForm preset source report

Reviewed 9 November 2026. These are reference presets from official notices. The user should follow the current notice for their exact exam, recruitment post, session, and application stage.

## NEET (UG) 2026 — National Testing Agency

The official 2026 Information Bulletin specifies a recent passport photo as JPG/JPEG, 10–200 KB, with a white background and face/ears visible; it gives signature as JPG/JPEG, 10–100 KB. The cited requirements do not prescribe pixel dimensions. AuraForm's 600 × 800 px portrait and 600 × 250 px signature dimensions are product defaults, not NTA requirements.

Official source: [NEET (UG) 2026 Information Bulletin](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/02/202602231394640855.pdf), pages 16–17.

## JEE (Main) 2026 — National Testing Agency

The official 2026 Information Bulletin specifies a recent colour passport photo as JPG/JPEG, 10–200 KB, with a white background and face/ears visible; the signature is JPG/JPEG, 10–100 KB. The cited requirements do not prescribe pixel dimensions. AuraForm's 600 × 800 px portrait and 600 × 250 px signature dimensions are product defaults, not NTA requirements.

Official source: [JEE (Main) 2026 Information Bulletin](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f8e59f4b2fe7c5705bf878bbd494ccdf/uploads/2025/11/202511021649722475.pdf), page 3.

## UPSC Recruitment Branch — Union Public Service Commission

The Recruitment Branch FAQ says each JPG photo/signature must be at most 40 KB. It specifies the photograph as 110 × 140 pixels (width × height) and signature as 140 × 110 pixels (width × height). The FAQ also says the signature should be on white paper in black ink. This is an official Recruitment Branch FAQ; consult the current recruitment advertisement and its upload instructions for the specific post.

Official source: [UPSC Recruitment Branch FAQs](https://upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Rectt_Branch_FAQs_0.pdf), question 16.

## Measurement and scope

AuraForm creates JPEG files and reports their exact byte count plus the equivalent KB before download. It targets just below the official maximum while preserving the selected pixel dimensions. If an output falls below a minimum, the app displays that fact. It does not edit identity, generate or replace a face, add a date/name label, or certify eligibility; users must review their own current form rules and output before submitting.
